## Overview

Singers Lyrics combines rich-text editing, lyric timing, and Apple Music playback. The apps share their document model and use platform-specific interfaces and playback services. Local libraries and optional iCloud Drive folder sync work without paid developer enrollment. The alternate CloudKit backend requires paid developer provisioning and is disabled in default builds.

Use these guides to understand the implementation, work with song documents, and validate changes. Begin with <doc:BuildingAndRunning> to set up a local build.
