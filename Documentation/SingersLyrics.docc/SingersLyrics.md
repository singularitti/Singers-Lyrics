## Overview

Singers Lyrics combines rich-text editing, lyric timing, and Apple Music playback. The apps share their document model and use platform-specific interfaces and playback services. Libraries stay on the device, and the personal-use workflow uses public native APIs without requiring paid developer enrollment.

Use these guides to understand the implementation, work with song documents, and validate changes. Begin with <doc:BuildingAndRunning> to set up a local build.
